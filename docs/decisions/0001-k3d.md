# 0001 – k3d instead of minikube, kind or EKS

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
I needed a Kubernetes cluster to learn on a MacBook Air M1 with 8 GB RAM, at no cost.

## Options
| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| EKS (AWS) | Closest to production | ~$73/month for the control plane alone |
| minikube | Popular, many tutorials | Single node by default, heavier |
| kind | Lightweight, common in CI | No Ingress controller included |
| **k3d** | Very light, multi-node in seconds, ships with Traefik and a NetworkPolicy controller | k3s differs slightly from upstream Kubernetes |

## Decision
k3d with 1 server and 1 agent.

## Consequences
- The whole cluster runs in about 1.5 GB of RAM.
- Traefik and NetworkPolicy work out of the box, which covered Phases 3 and 5 without extra installs.
- Manifests and the Helm chart are standard, so they can move to k3s on EC2 or to EKS later.
